What's This? This is a list of specifications that are written by experimentor, programmer and hobbyist UnnamedBruh. These specifications are independent exercises that can be adapted into an existing system without permission or attribution.
They are designed to:
  • Be nonintimidating: Some specifications specify a lot of edge cases or rules. Being intimidated by them is a common reaction, even for experts. UnnamedBruh's specifications attempt to avoid this issue.
  • Be plain: Some specifications are written in a formal, rigid tone that's easy to misunderstand. UnnamedBruh's specifications are written in plain, informal English while attempting to keep things precise.
  • Lack "big words": Some specifications use precise, obscure or dated terminology. UnnamedBruh tries to avoid most of them, unless they don't mind defining them.
